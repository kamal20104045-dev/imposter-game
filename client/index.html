How to Play 🎮

🕵️ The Roles

Normal Players: Know the secret word, try to identify the imposter

Imposter: Don't know the word, try to fake knowing it

📝 The Turn Phase (30 sec)

Each player takes turns entering a word. Players must decide if it's related to the secret word or if it's the imposter guessing.

🗳️ The Voting Phase (20 sec)

Everyone votes for who they think is the imposter. The player with the most votes is eliminated.

🏆 Winning

Normal Players Win: All imposters are eliminated

Imposters Win: Survive all rounds
